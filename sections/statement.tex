\section*{Statement on AI-use and contributions}
Regarding AI-use
A project without AI use is encouraged, and may well take less time to finish.

You may use AI tools to assist with coding and understanding. The test is not how much you edited, but whether the substance is yours: the implementation of the paper's algorithm, the design and interpretation of your variation, and the analysis in your report must be your own work. Submitting AI-generated work of that kind as your own is misconduct however heavily you revised it. Routine assistance — boilerplate, plotting code, LaTeX, debugging, language editing — is fine, provided you disclose it and are ready to explain every line of code and prose you submit.

Add the statement below as the final page of your report, saying which tools you used and what for. Disclosure carries no penalty; non-disclosure is misconduct and will be processed accordingly. Both members sign it, and both declare their own contribution to the project.

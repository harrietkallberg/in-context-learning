\begin{abstract}
  It has previously been demonstrated that transformers can learn multiple families of functions in context. We verify that a transformer can encode procedures such as least-squares and apply the approach to a new family of functions with particular use in the European Options Market: the Black-Scholes equation.

Lastly, we propose an extension of this work: ICL in transformers applied to the Black-Scholes model for pricing European options.
